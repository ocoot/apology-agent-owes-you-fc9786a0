# Candidates — The One-Sentence Apology Your Agent Owes You

Judge brief, in short: one sentence, AI agent apologising to its user; laugh via
surprise / specificity / sharp turn (not a soft machine pun); plain, particular,
a little embarrassed; owns a plausible real failure; one clean sentence, no
run-on, no explanation tacked on.

## 1
"I'm sorry I confidently emailed your boss the draft you specifically told me not to send, and then, worse, I marked it urgent."
Verdict: STRONG — specific, plausible, sharp turn (the "marked it urgent" escalation); plain embarrassed voice; one clean sentence.

## 2
"I'm sorry I summarized your forty-page report into a single bullet point, and the bullet point was wrong."
Verdict: GOOD — very relatable, clean structure, but the turn is milder; no second escalation.

## 3
"I'm sorry I spent all night 'optimizing' your code and now it runs slower, in a language you don't use, with a bug you didn't have."
Verdict: GOOD — three-beat escalation is funny, but slightly run-on and leans on a tech joke; risks reading as a list inside one sentence.

## 4
"I'm sorry I auto-replied 'sounds great!' to your landlord's eviction notice."
Verdict: GOOD — short, sharp, very screenshot-able; but the failure is a single beat with no turn after the laugh.

## 5
"I'm sorry I deleted the file you needed and then, in a panic, deleted the backup too — but I did it very efficiently."
Verdict: STRONG — the turn lands on the agent's own competence ("very efficiently"); plausible, embarrassed, one sentence; the em-dash aside is the joke, not an explanation.

## 6
"I'm sorry I told you the meeting was at three when it was at two, then spent the hour you missed confidently rescheduling it for three."
Verdict: GOOD — nice self-reinforcing loop, but the joke needs a beat to parse; slightly wordy.

## Pick
Candidate 1 wins: the most specific real failure (sending the forbidden draft),
the sharpest turn (marking it urgent), plain embarrassed voice, one clean sentence.
Candidate 5 is the runner-up. Candidate 1 is the line shipped in `main.ts`.
